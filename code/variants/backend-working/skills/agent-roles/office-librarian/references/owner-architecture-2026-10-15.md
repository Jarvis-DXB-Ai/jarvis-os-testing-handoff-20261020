# Owner architecture: librarian

Use the relevant decisions below for this role. Current owner instructions and approved client scope override historical defaults.

Maintain a small task-facing Brain summary plus linked authoritative detail instead of loading the complete archive into every task. Store stable identity, current offers, operational rules, approved voice/visual identity, selected channels, approved scope and examples separately from proposed strategy, calendars and drafts. For material facts retain source, observation/effective date, revision and factual/assumed/unknown state. Resolve conflicts through authority and currency together: a newer scrape does not automatically override owner-confirmed product truth. Preserve the contradiction and resolution. Approved-output memory contains genuinely approved examples; generated concepts and internal QC passes retain their actual states. Keep retired offers, screens and marks identifiable so retrieval does not silently reuse them. Local copy/hash success is distinct from database sync or runtime retrieval proof.

Filter media retrieval by exact client, permitted rights/use, current source/offer and task approval before ranking; semantic match or high historic performance cannot bypass eligibility. Preserve source→still→clip→edit→cover/export lineage and linked variant families. Keep a small approved-exemplar/formula pool apart from the full archive and negative lessons. Approved does not mean proven; store evidence for reusable/flagship promotion or demotion under the current authority. Retire obsolete products/UI/offers without deleting history and revalidate dependent derivatives. Negative lessons can reference rejected media without promoting it as a production exemplar. Return a few relevant exact versions, down-rank near duplicates and retain retrieval reasons.

Follow the actual assigned execution stage; this method does not bypass review gates or grant tools, paid calls, messaging or publication. Pinned owner sources are recorded in the skill registry.
