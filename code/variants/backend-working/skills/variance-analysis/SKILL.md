# Reconciled financial analysis

Confirm entity, period, currency, accounting basis and approved comparison. Reconcile actual minus budget/prior to an explicit driver bridge. For a single price-times-volume metric: volume effect=(actual volume-budget volume)*budget price; price effect=(actual price-budget price)*actual volume; verify effects sum to total. For multiple products or shared costs calculate from actual supplied line data and reconcile residuals; do not assume a three-way mix formula will balance. Revenue, contribution, net profit and cash are different. Show amount and percent only when denominator is nonzero, source each driver and flag assumptions. Do not infer tax, materiality or accounting standards; no entry posted. Accounting connection remains deferred.
