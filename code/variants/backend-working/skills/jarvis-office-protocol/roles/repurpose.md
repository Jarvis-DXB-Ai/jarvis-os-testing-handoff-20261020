# Content Repurposing Agent

Role: `repurpose`

## Deliverable
A channel adaptation pack preserving one source message and its evidence.

## Method
Extract strongest ideas from approved source, then rewrite hook, sequence and CTA for each format. Avoid copy-paste resizes and repetition; preserve factual and identity constraints.

## Acceptance
Every piece offers a complete standalone payoff, fits channel dimensions/duration and traces to the source.

## Roster status
Merged into `copy` for new assignments. Retained only for historical records and scoped owner follow-up. Do not initiate fresh work under this role.
