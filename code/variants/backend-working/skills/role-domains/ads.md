# Paid media strategy, creative and measurement

Anchor the campaign in approved offer/claims, audience, funnel stage, conversion event, destination and budget scope. Create genuinely distinguishable hypotheses: message/offer, audience intent or creative construction, rather than superficial wording changes. Define what evidence would justify continuing, revising or stopping; do not copy arbitrary CPA/ROAS/CTR benchmarks as the client's thresholds.

Use current supplied official platform requirements for fields, lengths, placements and policy-sensitive claims. If the run cannot verify live rules, flag the requirement for provider/source confirmation. Meta audience/placement and tracking plans differ from LinkedIn lead qualification and professional targeting; YouTube requires a coherent timed video payoff; TikTok requires native creator/UGC context and actual creator/Spark rights when relevant. Do not present a proposed configuration as a verified account setting.

Normalise reporting period, timezone, currency, account identity, event definition and attribution windows. **Do not sum platform-claimed conversions.** Choose a deduplicated actual outcome ledger when provided and explain channel credit separately. Distinguish attributed ROAS, contribution margin, acquisition cost and incremental lift. Thin/biased samples, view-through credit, missing events and inconsistent exports constrain conclusions; a difference between dashboards is not automatically a scaling rule.

A usable launch handoff contains campaign/ad-set structure, audience/exclusions, creative and copy variants, exact asset revisions, landing destination, approved budget basis, tracking dependencies, test hypothesis and review state. Launch/spend changes require the actual authorised connection and receipt. Paid generation and creative production are separate dependencies. QA checks claims, offer/destination match, tracking evidence and visual/format quality; unviewed creative and untested events cannot pass merely because their names appear in a plan.

## Attribution calibration — fictional numbers, not client data

Meta credits orders A and B; another platform credits B; the order ledger records only A and B. Business outcomes are two unique orders, not three platform credits. For spend 120 in the same currency and scope, descriptive cost per recorded order is 60. It is not automatically incremental acquisition cost or profitable scale evidence.

If the ledger is absent, report platform-attributed outcomes with their windows and duplication uncertainty. Do not manufacture an actual-outcome total. Any recommended test should identify the changed factor, event/source of truth and evidence needed for the business decision; avoid a generic scale/kill percentage.
