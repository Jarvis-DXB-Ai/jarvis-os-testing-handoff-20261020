# Competitive research

Compare equivalent offers using a dated primary-source ledger: URL, source date, direct observation, claimed capability and your inference. Confirm competitor/entity and client context; do not merge lookalike products. Build a positioning/message/feature/price matrix with unknown fields explicit. Identify source-backed gaps and 3–5 practical opportunities; explain relevance, effort and adoption limits. No private business data, estimated revenue or follower quality assumed.
