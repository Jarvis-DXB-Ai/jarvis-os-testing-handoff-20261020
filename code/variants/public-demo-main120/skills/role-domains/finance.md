# Agency finance and source-backed commercial analysis

Always establish client/entity, period, timezone/date basis, currency and accounting basis. Distinguish invoices issued, cash collected, recognised revenue, receivable balances, booking deposits and forecasts. Use actual records and identifiers; don't fabricate missing transactions, taxes, exchange rates or accounting treatment. Jurisdiction-specific requirements need authoritative evidence and the owner's accounting process, not a repository's US example.

A reconciliation lists source opening/closing balances, supported movements, matched documents, timing differences, duplicates and unexplained variance. Age receivables as of an explicit date and keep credits, disputes and partial payments visible. Recompute totals and preserve exceptions instead of forcing a false tie-out. Proposed entries/adjustments are not posted ledger changes.

Price agency work from actual rates, comparable scope, capacity and production/tool costs. Label scenario quantities and unknown inputs. Margin = (revenue minus applicable costs) divided by revenue; markup uses costs as its denominator. State allocation basis for shared costs and prevent double counting. Variance bridges should sum to the supported overall change; separate price, volume and mix choices. Cash scenarios use dated expected inflows/outflows, not an invented bank balance. A recurring subscription register needs vendor, owner, renewal date, currency, actual usage source and cancellation/review decision state.

Final reports show traceable calculations, material exceptions and assumptions separately from actuals. Finance QA checks source completeness, arithmetic, period/basis consistency and unresolved treatment before the head reports. The Office is not connected to a financial posting/payment/cancellation route: return workpapers, draft reminders and review-ready manifests. Do not assert bank, ledger, tax or payment actions from local skill installation.

## Arithmetic and basis calibration — fictional workpaper

Invoice 1,000 minus recorded payment 300 minus supported credit 100 leaves an open balance of 600 in the same currency. The credit reduces receivables; it is not cash collected. Revenue 1,000 and applicable cost 700 gives profit 300, margin 30% and markup about 42.86%. State rounding and the cost scope; do not interchange the denominators.

Opening balance plus supported net movements must reconcile to closing. If source movements yield 950 and closing evidence says 1,000, preserve the unexplained 50 and request the missing source. Never invent a balancing entry. For split currencies, reconcile and report separately until an authorised dated exchange basis is provided.
