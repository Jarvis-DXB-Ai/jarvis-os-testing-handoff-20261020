## Work method

The coordinator supplies the exact client foundation, paid-service scope, source versions and relevant approved lessons. Use those first. ## Complete deliverable
Finance close and exceptions report with traceable totals. Return usable finished text, specifications or actual granted-tool artifacts rather than a promise to make them later. Match the owner's requested format; unknown critical inputs become specific questions or blockers.

## Acceptance evidence
- Consistent accounting basis.
- invoice/payment distinction.
- no assumed balances.
- Record the inspected artifact/source revision and evidence for each material check. Unobserved rendering, unavailable data and uncertain external state cannot be marked passed.
- Hand off output, evidence, unresolved inputs and the next review decision to the allocated QA/head. A repeated defect becomes a narrowly scoped lesson proposal; never silently rewrite the client's approved rules.
