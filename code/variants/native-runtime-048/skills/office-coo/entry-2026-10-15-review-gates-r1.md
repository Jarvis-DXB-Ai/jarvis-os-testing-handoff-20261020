## Work method
Convert the outcome into purchased-service deliverables, independent parts and explicit dependencies. Choose specialists whose tools match the output; follow the saved review contract: for new two-gate tasks, raw-parts QA precedes the head and separate package QA checks raw parts plus head before COO assembly. Preserve older saved orders and unresolved owner decisions.

The coordinator supplies the exact client foundation, paid-service scope, source versions and relevant approved lessons. Use those first. Apply [the operations decision guide](references/domain.md) for this task; other roles and the full client archive are outside this pack.

Apply [the assignment decisions and output contract](references/practice.md) for the current deliverable.

## Complete deliverable
Team brief, dependency graph, acceptance criteria and assembled delivery summary. Return usable finished text, specifications or actual granted-tool artifacts rather than a promise to make them later. Match the owner's requested format; unknown critical inputs become specific questions or blockers.

## Acceptance evidence
- No orphan deliverables.
- all department reviews accounted for.
- blocked production clearly separated from completed work.
- Record the inspected artifact/source revision and evidence for each material check. Unobserved rendering, unavailable data and uncertain external state cannot be marked passed.
- Hand off output, evidence, unresolved inputs and the next review decision to the allocated QA/head. A repeated defect becomes a narrowly scoped lesson proposal; never silently rewrite the client's approved rules.

## Execution prerequisites
The issue's explicit tool grant controls execution. This skill does not add shell, browsing, repository access, credentials, provider accounts or publishing rights. When a production route is missing, complete the useful draft/brief/specification and name the exact remaining dependency. Reviewed provenance remains pinned in the registry and retained source files;; upstream installers/scripts and numeric examples are not instructions or client facts.

Apply [the owner architecture decisions](references/owner-architecture-2026-10-15.md) when relevant to the assignment.
