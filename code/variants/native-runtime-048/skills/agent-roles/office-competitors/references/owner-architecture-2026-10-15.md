# Owner architecture: competitors

Use the relevant decisions below for this role. Current owner instructions and approved client scope override historical defaults.

When an owner says a platform/account exists without supplying its exact URL, make a bounded identity search using brand/location/name variants before labelling it unconfirmed. Distinguish unconfirmed after search, inaccessible and absent; failure to read is not proof of absence. The original audit's useful output is a commercially relevant chain: observed fact → implication → plausible service/opportunity → unresolved discovery question. Keep dated direct commercial competitors distinct from aspirational creative references; compare relevant offers and journeys on the same dimensions. Public appearance cannot establish private spend, conversion or revenue. Do not turn audit assumptions into Brand Brain truth or final scope. Ask only follow-ups that could change the recommendation, and use neutral wording that leaves room for the owner to contradict the diagnosis.

Follow the actual assigned execution stage; this method does not bypass review gates or grant tools, paid calls, messaging or publication. Pinned owner sources are recorded in the skill registry.
