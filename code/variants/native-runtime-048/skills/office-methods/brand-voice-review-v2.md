# Brand and voice: independent quality appendix

Review the final scoped application against the exact approved foundation and voice examples. This is a critique, not permission to invent a new brand or repeat discovery.

- For each material claim, verify company/entity, offer, terminology, numbers and source. Prefer an accurate concrete benefit to unsupported superlatives, fabricated urgency or invented personal experience.
- Check whether vocabulary, sentence construction and degree of confidence match supplied client examples. Warm/premium/bold labels alone do not justify changes. A complaint reply may need a calmer tone than a launch post while keeping the same factual identity.
- Compare the opening promise, explanation/proof and next action. Remove formulaic wording only when it improves meaning; do not strip necessary qualifiers or flatten the client’s distinctive language into a universal style.
- When logos/visual brand applications are in scope, compare actual marks, proportions and crops to approved source assets. Text-only review cannot certify unseen artwork. A new proposed identity is different from a defect in applying the current one.

Return affected passage/element and artifact revision, the relevant approved rule/source, a complete replacement or concrete repair, and the evidence needed for recheck. Identify subjective alternatives with tradeoffs rather than call every preference a brand violation. Preserve the owner’s requested quantity and output format; a single finished caption does not need a complete brand manual.

Missing source evidence leaves a named claim/visual check unassessed. Feedback becomes a scoped revision criterion; propose a reusable client lesson only from an observed failure and reviewed repair. The original pinned source and licence are retained. It does not confer provider writes, publishing or a changed brand approval state.
