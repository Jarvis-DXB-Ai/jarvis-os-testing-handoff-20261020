Our starting point

Current local source already contains client-scoped foundations and task folders, service-based team selection, department QA, head reports, a COO queue, saved outputs, lesson review and connection-state UI. These should be reused. Current engine calls explicitly request fresh native sessions, and its catalog blocks requested capabilities without a verified route. A configured role, installed skill or simulated connection is separate from an agent completing high-quality real work.

The priority is reliable workflow execution, source-grounded review and measurable improvement. More avatars or agent names will not establish those outcomes.

What to avoid adopting unchanged

  • Demarly’s proprietary code, artwork, interface or private agent packs. The public patterns can inform our original implementation; its terms do not grant a software reuse licence.
  • Agent-count pricing as a measure of delivery capacity. Jarvis clients buy service outcomes and approved scope; model quota, tools and review capacity constrain execution.
  • Unreviewed automatic memory/skill changes. Keep candidate lessons, evidence, owner review and rollback before promotion.
  • Using a hosted provider as the canonical client Brain without proving export, client isolation, source provenance and recovery. Maintain the local shared folders and existing versioned records.
  • Generic whole-business autonomy as a promised result. Test the complete workflow and actual delivery receipts before enabling external actions.

Demarly’s landing page lists a Free tier and paid plans starting at $24.99/month; its published terms use immediate-charge wording while the landing page advertises trials. These are observed pricing pages, not a validated purchase or cost forecast. Its privacy policy also describes cloud/model processing, so its SaaS offering should not be treated as an all-local system. Plans · Terms · Processing disclosures

First DemoClientA workflow

Start with a manually triggered, source-backed content cycle. Use the existing proposal, current website/social work and confirmed brand references; select relevant competitor sources. Produce three briefs with evidence and distinct visual directions, route them through Marketing and Brand QA, then present the checked drafts for owner review. Save artefacts, decisions and revisions to the existing client folders. Verify realistic production tools separately before generating or publishing media.

  1. Client workflow map
  2. Dated sources
  3. Three briefs
  4. Department QA
  5. Owner decision
  6. Saved evidence
  7. Outcome and lesson

Pass conditions: correct client and sources, no unsupported product claims, usable on-brand briefs, actionable QA, retrievable saved files, and a revision that preserves accepted work. Replaying the same run must not create duplicates. Only then enable a recurring trigger or external delivery.

This supports the proposed Marketing umbrella with Social Media and Email specialist teams, each retaining appropriate QA. It is an organisational recommendation; the existing department IDs and roster have not been changed by this research.
