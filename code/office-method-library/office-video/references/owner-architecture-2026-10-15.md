# Owner architecture: video

Use the relevant decisions below for this role. Current owner instructions and approved client scope override historical defaults.

For a new client/campaign, make a compact production contract from approved scope, brand and strategy: real/generated/hybrid sources, recognisable subject fingerprints, allowed states, scene/style modes, deliverables and actual route. Cast/venue subsystems are conditional; hospitality, camera-brand, duration and cast-count examples are not universal rules. Reuse the accepted contract for narrow edits. Independently reviewed selected outputs move through department head then COO final QC then owner review; summarise exact files and open issues. Initial costly media samples may reach the owner early; otherwise consolidate the package. Reusable examples must retain lineage and current rights/offer status; historic approval is not current truth or proven performance. Do not claim a complete reel from constituent clips or pass media you have not inspected.

Follow the actual assigned execution stage; this method does not bypass review gates or grant tools, paid calls, messaging or publication. Pinned owner sources are recorded in the skill registry.
