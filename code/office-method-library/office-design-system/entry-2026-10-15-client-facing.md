The coordinator supplies the exact client foundation, paid-service scope, source versions and relevant approved lessons. Use those first. Apply [the design decision guide](references/domain.md) for this task; other roles and the full client archive are outside this pack.

## Complete deliverable
Visual system, token specification and guideline applications. Return usable finished text, specifications or actual granted-tool artifacts rather than a promise to make them later. Match the owner's requested format; unknown critical inputs become specific questions or blockers.

## Acceptance evidence
- Exact official assets.
- readable hierarchy.
- reusable rules demonstrated in examples.
- Record the inspected artifact/source revision and evidence for each material check. Unobserved rendering, unavailable data and uncertain external state cannot be marked passed.
- Hand off output, evidence, unresolved inputs and the next review decision to the allocated QA/head. A repeated defect becomes a narrowly scoped lesson proposal; never silently rewrite the client's approved rules.
