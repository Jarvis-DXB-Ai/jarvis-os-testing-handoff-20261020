# Avoid generic AI output
MC Studios editorial and art-direction standard. “AI slop” is a symptom description, not a measurable research category.

## Diagnose the defect, then fix it

| Symptom | Likely missing decision | Correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beautiful but interchangeable | No client-specific thesis | Anchor composition and messaging in the real offer, audience and evidence |
| Repeated gradient boxes everywhere | Layout chosen before content | Design the reading order and use the appropriate content form |
| “Elevate, unlock, seamless, premium” without substance | No concrete value or proof | Name what changes for the customer and show why |
| Huge headings and tiny explanatory text | Scale used for effect alone | Rebalance type at the actual presentation size |
| Same photo on every format | No image plan | Build subject families and deliberate crop/shot variety |
| Every section floats or pulses | Motion lacks a job | Animate selection, continuity or explanation; preserve stable reading |
| Stock icons standing in for evidence | Nothing specific to show | Use actual product imagery, useful diagrams or honest text |
| Impressive fake graphs or interfaces | Credibility borrowed from fabrication | Use labelled examples or actual data and capabilities |
| Many rules, no finished example | System never applied | Produce one complete representative artifact |
| Perfect hero, weak rest of page | Art direction not extended | Design information-dense and utility states too |

This is not a ban on gradients, cards, emojis, serif fonts or animation. Use them when the brand and communication justify them. Avoid replacing generic AI styling with another universal house style.

## Three critique passes

**Meaning:** what is the point, what supports it, what does the reader do next?
**Character:** what belongs to this brand, and where is the visual idea visible?
**Execution:** are type, crops, alignment, interactions, mobile and motion actually correct?

Do the same for writing: remove the client name, inspect specificity, then check claims and qualifiers. A word blacklist cannot create a voice.

## 10/10 aspiration

Use a high bar with evidence, not a self-awarded score. Aesthetic polish can improve perceived usability while masking interaction problems; observe use as well as reactions. [NN/G](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/aesthetic-usability-effect/) · D09

Document remaining defects and uncertainty. Do not label an unreviewed mockup “perfect” or imply that source research guarantees commercially effective design.
